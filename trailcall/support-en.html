Trailcall Support

Trailcall is an offline walkie-talkie for iPhone. It works only between iPhones running Trailcall, directly over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. No account, no internet and no mobile network are needed.

Frequently asked questions

Nobody is found

Open Trailcall on both iPhones, turn Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on in Control Center (no network needed), choose the same channel and PIN, and stay close together. If it still fails, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → Trailcall is on.

No sound

Hold the big button while talking; the first talk asks for Microphone permission. Turn up the volume on the listening phone and make sure it is not in a call.

Listening with the screen locked

Keep “Listen with screen off” on in Trailcall Settings and stay in the channel. Leaving the channel stops listening.

Range

Range depends on surroundings, obstacles and the phones; open terrain works best. Trailcall makes no fixed range promise.

SOS

SOS only alerts nearby iPhones that have Trailcall open. It does not call emergency services. If you can, call your local emergency number.

Plus purchase missing

Open Settings → Get Plus → “Restore purchase” with the Apple ID used for the purchase. Passes are one-time purchases and never renew automatically.
