1. Summary

  • 2 3 4 Games is a collection of offline mini games for 1 to 4 players on one device. There is no account, no sign-in, no chat, and we run no servers of our own.
  • Player nicknames, colours, match history and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • The app is designed to be suitable for children and families. All ads are non-personalised and limited to family-friendly content, for every user.
  • The app does not collect the device advertising ID, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data.
  • Google Firebase sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports so we can improve the games. They never contain player nicknames.
  • We do not sell personal data.

2. Data stored on your device

Player profiles (nickname, colour, character), match results, rivalry scores, cup history and settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device. Nicknames are whatever you type; you do not need to use real names. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share a result card (section 6).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between games (only when you move on to the next game; never during a game, never in the first session) and optional reward videos that unlock a locked or early-access game for the current session. Where required (for example in the EU/EEA/UK), Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform) shows a consent message; you can change your choice later in Settings → Ad preferences. Every ad request is marked as child-directed and under the age of consent, asks for non-personalised ads only, and is limited to content rated G. Under these settings Google does not use the request for personalised advertising or remarketing. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical data such as IP address and device type. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Buying Premium removes all ads and gives early access to new games.

4. Analytics and crash reports (Firebase)

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. They record which games are played, for how long, which buttons are used, and technical crash details (device model, OS version, app version). Collection of the advertising ID is turned off and ad-personalisation signals are disabled. Data is linked to a random app-instance identifier, not to you. Analytics data is kept by Google for 14 months; crash reports for 90 days.

Remote settings (Firebase Remote Config)

The app downloads settings from Google Firebase Remote Config, such as how often ads may appear, which games are new, and game timings. Only app and device technical data is involved; no nicknames or personal details are sent. Without internet the app uses the last downloaded or built-in settings.

Game vote

When the device is online, the game list may show "Which game should come next?". If you vote, only the chosen game's identifier is sent as an anonymous Firebase Analytics event. No name, profile or free text is sent. You can simply ignore the vote.

Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

After your first cup win the app may ask whether you want to hear about new games. Only if you agree (and allow notifications on Android) does Firebase Cloud Messaging create a notification token, used to subscribe the device to a language topic such as "new games in English". We do not store tokens on any server or send personalised notifications; we send at most one notification per week. You can turn notifications off at any time in Settings → New game notifications or in Android settings.

5. Purchases

The one-time "Premium" purchase is processed by Google Play (and, on iPhone, by the App Store). We never receive your payment details. The app stores only the fact that Premium is owned, and re-checks it with the store.

6. Sharing

At the end of a cup you can create a result card image. It contains the nicknames and scores shown on screen. It is shared only if you tap Share and choose an app; we do not upload it anywhere.

7. Children and families

The app may be used by children. We designed it to comply with the Google Play Families Policy and the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA): no personal information is knowingly collected from children, ads are non-personalised and family-friendly, links leaving the app and the purchase screen are behind a simple "press and hold" parental gate, and there are no social features. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Retention and deletion

You can erase all data on your device at any time in Settings → "Delete all data", or by uninstalling the app. To request deletion of analytics data associated with your app instance, email us; because we hold no account, include the date you used the app.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, UK, Brazil or California) you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your data. Most data stays on your device under your control; for the rest, contact us.

10. Changes

We will update this page if the app's data practices change, and change the effective date above.

Pengaturan jarak jauh (Firebase Remote Config)

Aplikasi mengunduh pengaturan dari Google Firebase Remote Config, seperti seberapa sering iklan boleh muncul, game mana yang baru, dan durasi permainan. Hanya data teknis aplikasi dan perangkat yang terlibat; tidak ada nama panggilan atau data pribadi yang dikirim. Tanpa internet, aplikasi menggunakan pengaturan terakhir yang diunduh atau pengaturan bawaan.

Voting game

Saat perangkat online, daftar game dapat menampilkan “Game mana minggu depan?”. Jika Anda memilih, hanya ID game yang dipilih yang dikirim sebagai peristiwa Firebase Analytics anonim. Tidak ada nama, profil, atau teks bebas yang dikirim. Anda boleh mengabaikan voting ini.

Notifikasi (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

Setelah kemenangan piala pertama Anda, aplikasi dapat bertanya apakah Anda ingin mendapat kabar tentang game baru. Hanya jika Anda setuju (dan mengizinkan notifikasi di Android), Firebase Cloud Messaging membuat token notifikasi yang digunakan untuk mendaftarkan perangkat ke topik bahasa seperti “game baru dalam bahasa Indonesia”. Kami tidak menyimpan token di server mana pun dan tidak mengirim notifikasi yang dipersonalisasi; kami mengirim paling banyak satu notifikasi per minggu. Anda dapat mematikan notifikasi kapan saja di Pengaturan → Notifikasi game baru atau di pengaturan Android.

6. Berbagi

Di akhir piala, Anda dapat membuat gambar kartu hasil. Gambar ini berisi nama panggilan dan skor yang tampil di layar. Gambar hanya dibagikan jika Anda mengetuk Bagikan dan memilih aplikasi; kami tidak mengunggahnya ke mana pun.

7. Anak-anak dan keluarga

Aplikasi ini dapat digunakan oleh anak-anak. Kami merancangnya agar mematuhi Kebijakan Keluarga Google Play dan Undang-Undang Perlindungan Privasi Online Anak AS (COPPA): tidak ada informasi pribadi yang dengan sengaja dikumpulkan dari anak-anak, iklan tidak dipersonalisasi dan ramah keluarga, tautan yang keluar dari aplikasi dan layar pembelian dilindungi gerbang orang tua sederhana "tekan dan tahan", dan tidak ada fitur sosial. Jika Anda yakin seorang anak telah memberikan informasi pribadi kepada kami, hubungi kami dan kami akan menghapusnya.

8. Penyimpanan dan penghapusan

Anda dapat menghapus semua data di perangkat kapan saja melalui Pengaturan → “Hapus semua data”, atau dengan mencopot pemasangan aplikasi. Untuk meminta penghapusan data analitik yang terkait dengan instalasi aplikasi Anda, kirimkan email kepada kami; karena kami tidak menyimpan akun, sebutkan tanggal Anda menggunakan aplikasi.
