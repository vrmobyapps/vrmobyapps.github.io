Privacy Policy

Text Story Maker: Chat Story (Android, package com.vrmoby.textstorymaker) · Publisher: VR Moby · Effective: 7 November 2026

Text Story Maker lets you write fictional chat stories and export them as videos or screenshots. There is no account and no server of ours: your stories never leave your phone unless you share them yourself.

1. What stays on your device

Stories, character names, message text, photos you pick, and exported videos are stored only on your device. Photos are chosen with the Android photo picker; the app does not ask for access to your gallery, camera, microphone, contacts or location. We cannot see or recover this content. If you uninstall the app, it is deleted. You can back up a story as a .tsm file and keep that file wherever you choose.

3. Consent and your choices

In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform appears before any ad is requested. You can change your choice later in Settings › Privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Premium removes ads entirely.

4. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it with anyone except the service providers listed above, acting for the purposes listed above.

5. Retention and deletion

Analytics data is kept for no longer than 14 months and crash reports for 90 days. Because we hold no account, we cannot link this data to you by name. To ask for deletion of analytics data tied to your app instance, email us with the approximate install date; uninstalling the app also stops all collection.

6. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children.

7. Security

Data sent to Google's services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

8. Fictional content

All stories created with the app are fiction. Free exports carry the line "Made with Text Story Maker · Fictional story". Please do not present stories as real conversations or use real people's names or photos without permission.
