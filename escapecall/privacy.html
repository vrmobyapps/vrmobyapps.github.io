Escape Call – Privacy Policy

Publisher: VR Moby · App: Escape Call: Safety Call Timer (com.vrmoby.escapecall) · Last updated: 11 November 2026

Escape Call lets you schedule a simulated incoming call to yourself. It has no account and no server of its own. This policy explains what stays on your phone and what is shared with third-party services.

Data that stays on your device

  • Callers you create: name, number, label, photo, ringtone choice, vibration and theme.
  • Voice: recordings you make with the microphone and audio files you pick.
  • Scheduled calls and app settings.

These are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We never upload, sell or share them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the countdown and the incoming call.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): so the call rings at the exact time you set.
  • Full-screen notifications: to show the call screen over the lock screen.
  • Microphone: only when you tap record to create a voice for a caller. The recording stays on your device.
  • Vibration, wake lock, run at startup: to vibrate, keep the call screen on and restore scheduled calls after a restart.
  • Internet / network state / advertising ID: for ads in the free version, purchases and anonymous app statistics.

Escape Call does not read your contacts, call log, SMS, photos library, location or camera. When you pick a photo or a contact, Android's own picker gives the app only the single item you chose.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob (free version only): shows banner ads and, only when you tap "Watch an ad" on the Pro screen, a rewarded video that unlocks one Pro feature for 24 hours. It may use the advertising ID, IP address and device information. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first; you can change it later in Settings › Ad privacy choices. How Google uses data from ads.
  • Google Play Billing: processes the one-time Pro purchase. We do not receive your payment details.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google): anonymous usage events (for example "a call was scheduled", "a call rang late by 7 seconds", device model and Android version) and crash reports. These events never include caller names, numbers, photos or recordings. Firebase privacy.

Children

Escape Call is not directed to children under 13 and is not part of the Designed for Families program.

Not an emergency service

Escape Call does not place real phone calls and is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Your choices

  • Delete a caller, recording or photo inside the app, or uninstall the app to delete everything.
  • Change ad consent in Settings › Ad privacy choices, or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Buy Pro to remove ads.
